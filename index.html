WHAT IS BLOOM?

Art can open a door to understanding.

The BLOOM Project is an art-centered domestic violence education and advocacy initiative. Through visual art, storytelling, educational programming, and community engagement, BLOOM explores experiences that can remain hidden before, between, and after incidents of abuse.

Our goal is to make difficult experiences visible, create opportunities for recognition and reflection, and connect people with information and resources that can support safety, healing, freedom, and growth.

THE BOTANICAL CYCLE OF ABUSE

The rose doesn't begin with the thorn.

BLOOM uses botanical imagery to help people explore patterns that may appear within abusive relationships. The artwork creates an entry point for learning, reflection, and conversation.

ABOUT BLOOM

Created at the intersection of lived experience, public health education, advocacy, and art.

The BLOOM Project recognizes domestic violence as an issue that can affect individuals, families, children, communities, and public health. By using art as an entry point, BLOOM creates another way to begin conversations that can otherwise be difficult to start.

Founded in 2026 by Christine Smith-Thorpe, MPH. Queens, New York. Serving New York State with in-person and online engagement.
