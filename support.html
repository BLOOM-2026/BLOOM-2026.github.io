Help BLOOM Grow

The BLOOM Project brings art, education, and advocacy together to illuminate domestic violence, amplify creative voices, and nurture pathways toward healing. There are different ways to be part of that work.

For individuals

Contribute to BLOOM

Interested in helping BLOOM bring exhibitions, educational materials, and community programming to life? We'd welcome a conversation about how you'd like to contribute.

Contact us first to discuss possible contributions. This page does not process payments.

For businesses and organizations

Become a BLOOM Sponsor

Partner with BLOOM to help make art-centered education and advocacy more visible and accessible. We welcome inquiries about sponsoring exhibitions, artist programming, educational resources, and community events.

Possible sponsorship recognition may include acknowledgment on our website, exhibition materials, event signage, or the future digital gallery book, depending on the sponsorship agreement.

Request Sponsorship Details

Sponsorship benefits, amounts, and deliverables are discussed individually and confirmed in writing.

Important information: The BLOOM Project is currently an independently operated project, not a registered 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Contributions are not represented as tax-deductible charitable gifts. Sponsorship and contribution arrangements are subject to applicable requirements.
